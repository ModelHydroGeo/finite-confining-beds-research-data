# Numerical methods represented by the released results

The associated manuscript couples a reservoir heat-transport formulation to one-dimensional normal conduction in its confining beds. Three physical bed representations appear in the released data: a semi-infinite half-space, a finite bed with an insulated remote boundary, and a finite bed with a maintained remote temperature. The finite-bed results use the causal modal diffusion formulation described in the manuscript.

The local-verification files contain imposed-step and pulse histories, closed reservoir--bed capacity tests, and resolution studies. The Barends files contain depth-mean temperature profiles for analytical, modal, scalar and explicit comparison representations. The advective-cooling files record the temperature and global-energy histories for the three confining-bed representations, with independent finite-diffusion reference observations for the finite cases. The geothermal-doublet files record production-temperature histories, energy inventories and audits for a scalar half-space and two finite insulated-bed thicknesses.

The package provides output data only. Numerical methods, physical assumptions, boundary conditions and scenario identifiers are retained in the CSV columns and documented in the associated manuscript; runnable model configurations are deliberately not released here.
