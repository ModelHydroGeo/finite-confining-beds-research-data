# Data availability statement

The processed numerical datasets underlying the quantitative figures and reported comparisons in the associated manuscript are supplied in this research-data package. They comprise local verification histories, Barends benchmark profiles and comparison metrics, advective-cooling histories, and geothermal-doublet production and energy results.

A persistent repository identifier and publication bibliographic details will be added when established. No DOI is assigned at the time of this package release.

The package does not contain simulation software, source code, executables, input files, model configurations, proprietary files, or full three-dimensional meshes. Those materials are outside the scope of this data release. See `DATA_MANIFEST.csv` and `documentation/data_provenance.md` for file-level provenance and `FIGURE_DATA_MAPPING.csv` for the relationship to the manuscript figures.
